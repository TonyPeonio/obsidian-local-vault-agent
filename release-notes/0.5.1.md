The clean-up that used to fail now passes every run, a rejected change really stops the agent, and the benchmark grew to 19 scenarios.

**Fixed**
- **A rejected change ends the turn properly.** After you reject a diff the agent refuses every further write in that turn, tells you what was rejected and asks what you want instead. Before, the model sometimes said "Done" after a rejection or retried a variant of the same edit.
- **Surveys the model could misread.** `list_sections` with `containing: ["## Capture"]` and `heading: "Capture"` returned nothing (the heading line is not part of a section's body), which the model took as "no Capture sections exist". The text is now treated as the heading, and an empty match says "N sections exist, but none contains X". Sections that contain every requested text are marked `→ all` with a line telling the model to act on them without reading them first; the 9B model used to re-read all 40 sections to "check".
- **Clean-ups across many notes** no longer ask for one giant `edit_notes` call: at most 8 edits per call, one call per note. An 18-edit call made the model emit nothing at all.
- **Process my daily notes** requires the Sources section in every topic note it writes.
- An attached note that only half answers a question is followed up with a search instead of "the note does not mention it".
- The Sources footer also covers answers taken straight from `search_passages` results, naming the hits the answer mentions.

**Measured**
- 19 scenarios, 115 checks (new: a follow-up turn that must use the conversation, a question with no answer in the vault with a check for invented links, a rejected change). Final build, three runs of the defaults: 113, 113, 112 in 151–243 s (the 0.5.0 code scored 87–92 of the old 96 checks on the same night); the clean-up now surveys once and edits once in most runs (it passed 0 of 3 before). Model time is 90–97% of wall time. `qwen3.5:4b` scores 108 in under two minutes and is now the recommended 8 GB model. 64k context and thinking on did not beat the defaults.
